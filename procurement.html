Medical, mobility and orthopaedic supplies.

Send the RFQ, the tender document or your specification with the reference and closing date. We confirm whether we can quote, identify any line outside our scope, and say so before the deadline rather than after it.

Supplies overview

What a supplies quotation includes

  • Specification sheet for the exact item quoted
  • Manufacturer and country of origin
  • HS or UNSPSC classification where applicable
  • Warranty, spares and documentation terms
  • Full delivery price, delivery time and delivery terms
  • Quotation validity date and payment terms

Order size and lead time

  • No minimum order value; manufacturer pack sizes apply
  • Single cartons to full container loads
  • Items held in Lagos: indicative 3–7 working days
  • Air freight import: indicative 2–4 weeks
  • Sea freight import: indicative 6–10 weeks
  • Binding date stated on the dated quotation

Enquiries

Procurement routes.

Requests for quotation · supplies

Send the items, quantities, delivery city, delivery terms and deadline. We reply with a dated quotation or confirm that a category is outside our scope.

Tenders and prequalification

Supplier registration, vendor onboarding and prequalification questionnaires for either route. Include the reference, the country and the submission date.

Requests for proposal · mapping

Formal RFPs and terms of reference. We will confirm whether we intend to bid and identify any conflict before receiving confidential material.

Subconsultancy and joint bids

Geospatial or digital scope within a larger engineering, planning or technology bid. We can provide a scope note and personnel input.

Institutional partnerships

Programmes involving supplies, spatial data, accessible infrastructure or capacity building. Early-stage collaboration is welcome where roles are defined.

Document requests

Supplier dossier, certificate of incorporation, capability statements, method summaries, personnel profiles and clarifications. Only documents that exist will be issued.

What we will not provide: fictitious certificates, audited accounts, client references we have not been given permission to share, or approved-supplier claims for institutions where no approval exists.
