Waide Mobility.

Waide Mobility is a Lagos-based consultancy. We map buildings and sites, build digital maps and routes, and help teams choose and manage mapping tools.

Enquiries

Procurement routes.

Expressions of interest

Shortlisting, prequalification and supplier registration requests. Include the scope, the country and the submission date.

Requests for proposal

Formal RFPs and terms of reference. We will confirm whether we intend to bid and identify any conflict before receiving confidential material.

Subconsultancy and joint bids

Geospatial or digital scope within a larger engineering, planning or technology bid. We can provide a scope note and personnel input.

Institutional partnerships

Programmes involving spatial data, accessible infrastructure or capacity building. Early-stage collaboration is welcome where roles are defined.

Technology partnerships

Integration, data exchange or platform collaboration. We will assess fit against client confidentiality and data ownership obligations.

Document requests

Capability statements, method summaries, personnel profiles and clarifications. Only documents that exist will be issued.

What we will not provide: fictitious certificates, audited accounts, client references we have not been given permission to share, or approved-supplier claims for institutions where no approval exists.
