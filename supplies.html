How we work

Ordered to meet your specification.

Waide supplies medical consumables and equipment, mobility equipment and orthopaedic supports to organisations that buy on specification. There is no warehouse, no catalogue price list and no online checkout. Each order is sourced and quoted individually.

We do not publish prices or products we cannot support. Send a specification and we will confirm availability, total price, delivery time and the documents included in the quotation. You can also browse the shop by category first.

Who this is for

  • Hospitals, clinics and diagnostic centres
  • NGOs, missions and humanitarian programmes
  • Schools, universities and training institutions
  • Government agencies and public buyers

How orders are quoted

  • Against your specification, not a fixed list
  • With manufacturer and country of origin
  • Full delivery price, delivery time and delivery terms
  • In NGN or USD, as you prefer

Categories

Three things we source.

Each category is quoted from a written specification. Brand, model and packaging are agreed before an order is placed.

Category 01

Medical consumables & equipment

Dressings, syringes, gloves, giving sets, diagnostic sets and ward equipment. Send the specification you already use — a brand, a size range, a standard — and we quote against it.

  • Dressings, bandages and wound care
  • Syringes, needles and infusion consumables
  • Examination gloves and protective wear
  • Diagnostic sets and ward equipment

Where a specification is ambiguous, we ask before quoting rather than substitute silently.

Category 02

Mobility equipment

Manual and transit wheelchairs, walking frames, crutches and transfer aids. Quantities are usually set by the user group you serve, so tell us the setting: ward, school, clinic or community programme.

  • Manual and transit wheelchairs
  • Walking frames and rollators
  • Crutches and walking sticks
  • Transfer boards and belts

Assessment, fitting and adjustment are clinical tasks. We supply to the specification your clinician or therapist provides.

Category 03

Orthopaedic supports

Braces, walking boots, collars, slings, cushions and seating supports, supplied against a clinician’s specification. Sizing and product type are confirmed in writing before dispatch.

  • Knee, ankle and wrist braces
  • Walking boots and post-operative footwear
  • Cervical collars and arm slings
  • Pressure-relief and seating cushions

We do not diagnose, prescribe or fit orthoses. The specification comes from the treating clinician.

Process

From specification to delivery.

Five stages, each with something you can check. No stage is skipped to make a price look better.

  1. Specification

    You send the item, quantity, delivery city and deadline. A brand, standard or sample helps; if you have none, we ask what the product has to do.

    OutputWritten requirement and open questions

  2. Quotation

    We confirm availability, manufacturer, country of origin and delivery time, then give you the total order price.

    OutputQuotation with documents

  3. Confirmation

    You confirm the specification, quantity and delivery terms. Any substitution is agreed in writing before the order proceeds.

    OutputConfirmed order record

  4. Sourcing and shipping

    We place the order with the manufacturer or distributor and keep you informed about dispatch and customs clearing.

    OutputDispatch and shipping updates

  5. Delivery and documents

    Goods are delivered to the agreed address with the packing list, invoice and the documents named in the quotation.

    OutputDelivery record and warranty terms

Questions

Before you send a specification.

Do you have a catalogue or price list?

No standing catalogue or price list is published. Prices move with manufacturer availability and freight, so quoting from a fixed list would mislead. Send a specification and you get a dated quotation instead.

Can you respond to a tender or RFQ?

Yes. Send the document, the reference and the closing date. If a mandatory requirement is outside our capacity, we will tell you before the deadline rather than after.

Can you supply samples before an order?

Sometimes. Sample availability and cost depend on the manufacturer, so it is quoted as a separate line. Samples are not treated as free unless the quotation says so.

Do you deliver outside Lagos?

Yes, by quotation. Delivery is priced to the city and country you name, with the delivery terms stated on the quotation. We do not promise a date we have not confirmed.

Who handles customs and clearing?

It depends on the delivery terms you choose. We state who is responsible for transport, insurance, import duty and customs clearing in every quotation.
