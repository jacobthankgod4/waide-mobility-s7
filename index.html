Why it matters

The sale is lost before it ever reaches your shop.

Questions

Common questions

What does Waide do?
Waide maps a venue into a digital twin, adds navigation visitors open from a QR scan, and turns the searches and routes into reports for the operator. Visitors get directions. The operator gets a record of what people looked for and where they went.
Is this just an indoor navigation company?
No. Navigation is how visitors use it. The data it produces is what operators buy: zone performance, product demand, drop-off and lease evidence they cannot get from a POS or footfall counter alone.
What is live at Novare Lekki right now?
A 22,000 m² digital twin covering the ground floor, Level 1 and Parking Level A; 900 Shoprite shelves mapped at shelf level; 150+ store footprints; 1,000 parking bays; and QR-activated web navigation. It is a six-month pilot, not a commercial rollout. Read the full status.
Do visitors need to download an app?
No. A QR code at the entrance opens Waide in the phone browser. Nothing to install and no account to create, which matters where phone storage and data are limited.
Who owns the shopper data?
The venue owns the data collected in its building. We remove personal identifiers, never sell personal data, and set retention terms in the contract.
What does a deployment cost?
Three routes: a fixed-fee pilot on one venue, an indicative per-venue platform fee (from US$399/month), or a portfolio agreement for three or more sites. See how pricing works, or request a walkthrough for a scoped quote.
