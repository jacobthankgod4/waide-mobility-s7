Questions

The things buyers ask first.

What does Waide actually do?
Waide maps a large indoor venue into a digital twin, adds navigation that visitors use from a QR scan, and turns the resulting movement and search data into reports for the operator. In short: better wayfinding for visitors, better visibility for the people running the building.
Is this just an indoor navigation company?
No. Navigation is the interface that generates the data. The commercial proposition is the spatial intelligence underneath it: zone performance, product demand, drop-off and lease evidence that a venue cannot get from its POS or footfall counter alone.
What is live at Novare Lekki right now?
A 22,000 m² digital twin covering the ground floor, Level 1 and Parking Level A; 900 Shoprite shelves mapped at shelf level; 150+ store footprints; 1,000 parking bays; and QR-activated web navigation. It is an active six-month pilot, not a completed commercial rollout. Read the full status.
Do visitors need to download an app?
No. A QR code at the entrance opens Waide in the phone browser. There is no installation, no account and no friction, which matters in markets where storage and data are limited.
Who owns the shopper data?
The venue operator owns the first-party behavioural data generated in their property. We anonymise individual identities, do not sell personal data, and define the reporting and retention terms in the deployment agreement.
What does a deployment cost?
Three routes: a fixed-fee pilot on one venue, an indicative per-venue platform fee (from US$399/month), or a portfolio agreement for three or more sites. See how pricing works, or request a walkthrough for a scoped quote.
