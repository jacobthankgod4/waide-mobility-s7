Active pilot · Lagos, Nigeria

Novare Lekki Mall: what the pilot covers.

A six-month pilot across 22,000 m² of mall in Lekki, Lagos. This page records the scope, what is live today, and the measures the pilot will publish. It does not present projections as results.

Why this venue

A hard building, not a showroom.

Novare Lekki is a working mall with an anchor supermarket, a dense tenant mix and a car park that feeds visitors into the building. If navigation works here, it works in most African retail.

900 Shoprite shelves

Product-level search inside the anchor supermarket. Aisle and shelf destinations, not just store names.

150+ store footprints

Each tenant is a destination with its own entrance, level and opening hours.

1,000 parking bays

The journey starts in the car park. Parking Level A is mapped as part of the route, not treated as an afterthought.

Venue figures are supplied by the mall and used as deployment scope, not as performance claims. They describe the size of the problem, not the size of the result.

Deployment status

What is live, what is in progress, what has not started.

The pilot deploys the same three layers sold commercially: a digital twin, a visitor navigation interface, and an operator dashboard. The table separates what a visitor can use today from what is still being built.

Novare Lekki pilot deployment status
Component Status Detail
Digital twin Live Ground floor, Level 1 and Parking Level A mapped, including tenant footprints and facilities.
Visitor navigation Live QR entry points open the web app in the browser. No app install. Store, shelf, counter, lift and toilet destinations.
Operator dashboard Live Search log, zone traffic and drop-off views available to the venue team.
Baseline capture In progress Pre-launch baseline for the pilot metrics, so change can be compared with something.
Accessibility route validation In progress Step-free paths checked on site. This is a route check, not a third-party accessibility audit.
Measured outcome report Not started Published at the end of the pilot window. No commercial result is claimed before then.
Named partner quotation Pending permission No public quotation has been approved. See the note below.

Measurement plan

The numbers this pilot is built to produce.

These are the measures agreed for the evaluation. Each has a definition, a baseline captured before launch, and a reporting point. Definitions are fixed in advance so the result cannot be chosen after the fact.

Pilot metrics and definitions
Metric Definition Baseline First read
Search completion Share of product or store searches that end with the visitor reaching the destination. Pre-launch observation Day 90
Drop-off zone The zone where abandoned routes cluster most often. Pre-launch observation Day 90
Top unmet searches Searches with no matching destination or no completed route, ranked by volume. Not applicable Day 30
QR scan to route Share of QR scans that start a navigation route within the session. Not applicable Day 30
Zone dwell change Change in time spent in a zone, compared with the same period before launch. Pre-launch observation Day 90
Route completion time Median time from route start to arrival for the same destination type. Pre-launch observation Day 90

No results are published yet. When the evaluation closes, the measured figures will be added to this page and to the evidence register, with the sample size and the period they cover. Until then, the US$5–7M figure on the homepage remains a model, not a pilot result.

Limits

What this case study does not claim.

  • It is not a commercial rollout. One venue is live; no multi-site deployment is claimed.
  • It does not claim a measured sales lift, basket increase or rent uplift. The pilot is designed to test those, not to prove them in advance.
  • It does not claim third-party accessibility certification. Accessible routes are mapped, but no external audit has been completed.
  • It does not include a customer quotation, because none has been approved for publication.
  • It does not use venue size as a performance result. 22,000 m² describes the deployment, not the outcome.

Evaluation

What the end-of-pilot report will contain.

Scope and method

What was mapped, how routes were built, what changed during the pilot, and what was excluded.

Measured metrics

Each metric above, with its baseline, sample size, period and any caveats about data quality.

What did not work

Routes that failed, zones that underperformed, and the operational issues the venue team had to absorb.

Operator actions

What the venue changed as a result: signage, directory edits, tenant placement, staff routing.

Cost and rollout

What a second venue would cost, what would be reused, and what would have to be rebuilt.

Open questions

The questions the pilot could not answer, stated plainly rather than folded into a conclusion.
