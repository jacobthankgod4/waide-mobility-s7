Wayfinding

From the car park to the shelf.

A QR code at the entrance opens a map in the visitor's phone browser. They search for a store, a product or an exit, and follow a route. Nothing to install.

The job

Four questions a visitor asks at the door.

Wayfinding is not a map on a wall. It is a short, correct answer to one of these, on the device the person already has.

Where is this store?

Search by name, brand or category, then get a route to the unit and the entrance closest to it.

Where is this product?

Where the venue supplies shelf data, the route ends at the aisle and shelf rather than the front of the store.

Is there a way without stairs?

Step-free routes use lifts and ramps. The toggle is visible before the route starts, not buried in settings.

How do I get back out?

Exits, lifts, toilets and parking are on the same map, so the return trip is not a second problem.

What is included

The visitor experience, in full.

Search

Stores, products, facilities, counters and exits, with synonyms and local-language terms where supplied.

Turn-by-turn routes

Text steps and a moving position on the map, built from walkable paths rather than a straight line.

Step-free toggle

Lifts and ramps only. Useful for wheelchairs, pushchairs, trolleys and anyone carrying something heavy.

Local languages

Interface and destination labels can run in local African languages as well as English.

Multi-level routing

Ground floor, upper levels and parking are one route, with the level change stated explicitly.

QR entry

One code per entrance. Works in the phone browser, which matters where storage and data are limited.

Measurement

What a wayfinding pilot measures.

Four of the six pilot metrics apply directly to wayfinding. Each is defined before launch, with a baseline and a sample size.

Metric Definition
Search completion Share of searches that end with the visitor reaching the destination.
Drop-off zone The zone where abandoned routes cluster. This is where the building loses people.
QR scan to route Share of scans that start a route in the same session.
Route completion time Median time from route start to arrival, for the same destination type.

The full six-metric definition list is on the platform page. Results are published with the period they cover, not as a single headline number.

Questions

Common questions

Does the visitor need an app or an account?

No. A QR code opens the map in the phone browser. There is no install, no login and no loyalty sign-up.

Does it work without Bluetooth or beacons?

Yes. The visitor picks a destination and follows the route. The map does not need to know exactly where they are standing, which is also why it needs no beacons.

How do you handle a store that moves?

The venue tells us, and the map and directory are updated. Tenancy and directory changes are included for the term of the agreement.

Can the venue use its own branding?

Yes. The visitor experience carries the venue name and colours. The operator dashboard is separate.

What happens to the data?

The venue owns the data collected in its building. Waide removes personal identifiers, does not sell personal data, and agrees retention terms in the contract.

Walk the route on your own floor plan.

Send a plan and a floor count. We will show you where the map already has answers, and where the building is currently losing people.
