Image credits and disclosure

Which images on this site are real project records, which are stock photographs, and which are illustrations.

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Disclosure

What the images do and do not show.

Product and business-area photographs are stock images. They illustrate a category; they do not show Waide stock, the exact products we would quote, our suppliers, clients or beneficiaries. No product is held in stock, so no photograph should be read as a catalogue item.

Some service visuals are illustrations or AI-generated images. They explain a concept. They are not photographs of a completed Waide deployment unless the page says so.

The Novare Lekki Mall captures are real project records. They come from the project described on the project page. They are the only images on the site presented as Waide project evidence.
