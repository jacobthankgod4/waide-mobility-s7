Before you write

Common questions.

How quickly can a project start?

After a scoping conversation and a written scope note. Small assessments can often start within a few weeks; larger builds depend on the current schedule.

Do you sign NDAs?

Yes, where required. Send the NDA with the enquiry and we will review it before any confidential material is shared.

Can you provide a formal proposal?

Yes. We usually start with a short scope note covering deliverables, exclusions, assumptions, acceptance criteria and cost, then expand it into a proposal if the client needs one.

Do you publish client work?

Only with written permission and only at the level of detail the client has approved. The Novare project is published because the venue and project have been described publicly; commercial terms are not disclosed.

Is the form stored anywhere?

No. It assembles a mailto link and opens your email application. The website does not receive or store the message.
