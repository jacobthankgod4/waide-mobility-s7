Before you send

Three things make the first call useful.

01

A floor plan

PDF, CAD or a clear photo. If the plan is out of date, say so and we will check the space first.

02

Who uses the building

Shoppers, patients, passengers, students or staff. That decides what the map has to answer.

03

What you would count as working

Fewer people asking for directions, fewer searches ending in nothing, or shorter routes. Pick one before launch.

Questions

Common questions

What does a pilot cost?

It is a fixed fee quoted after a floor-plan review, because the work depends on levels, units and whether product data exists. Platform fees for a full deployment start at US$399 a month; see pricing.

How long before the map is live?

A single venue usually takes a few weeks from receiving usable plans. Multi-level or older buildings take longer. The scope note gives a date before you commit.

Do you sign an NDA?

Yes, if you need one before sending plans. Send your template with the first email and we will return it signed.

We have no digital floor plans. Can you still quote?

Yes. We can work from a measured survey, which is a separate quoted piece of work. Tell us in the first email and we will include it in the scope note.

Can we visit the live pilot?

The Novare Lekki deployment is a six-month pilot at a working mall, so visits are arranged with the venue. Ask on the call and we will check what is possible.

Not ready to talk?

Read the pilot scope and the evidence register first. Both are written for someone checking the numbers, not skimming a brochure.
