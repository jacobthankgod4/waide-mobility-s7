How ordering works

Browse, specify, quote.

Three steps, no cart. The quotation is the order document, and it states exactly what is being supplied.

01

Send the specification

Item description, brand or standard, quantity, delivery city and deadline. A tender reference helps if you have one.

02

We confirm the detail

Availability, manufacturer and country of origin, classification where applicable, warranty and delivery terms.

03

You receive a dated quote

Full delivery price, delivery time, validity date and payment terms — or a clear statement that the category is outside our scope.

Why no prices on this page: prices move with manufacturer availability and freight. A published figure would be stale before you read it. A dated quotation is the accurate document.
