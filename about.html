About

We map buildings so people can find their way.

Waide Mobility is a small team working on indoor maps, visitor navigation and floor-level reporting. We started in 2019 and our first live deployment is a mall in Lekki, Lagos.

Why we exist

Buildings are hard to find your way around.

Most large buildings in African cities were not designed to explain themselves. A visitor arrives, reads a sign, and asks someone. Staff spend part of every day giving directions. The operator sees people at the door and a sales total at the end, and very little in between.

We map the building, put the map in the visitor's browser, and record what people search for and where they stop. The map solves the visitor's problem. The record solves the operator's.

How we work

Four rules we keep.

Pilot before rollout

One venue, agreed measures, a written evaluation. If it does not work, we say so and you have lost a pilot, not a portfolio.

Claims carry labels

Measured, observed, modelled or an industry estimate. Every number on this site says which one it is, and the basis is public.

Accessibility is default

Step-free routes and screen-reader support are part of the platform, not an upgrade. Our UI/UX co-designer is visually impaired.

Local languages

Navigation should work in the languages people speak. We build for that from the start, not as a translation layer later.

Where we are

One live deployment, and a long way to go.

The Novare Lekki Mall pilot is our reference deployment: 22,000 m² across the ground floor, Level 1 and Parking Level A, with 900 Shoprite shelves indexed and more than 150 store footprints. It is a six-month evaluation, not a commercial rollout.

Our Kenya work runs through the Najimudu Empowerment Initiative, which focuses on accessibility and inclusion. It is a partnership, not a deployment, and we describe it that way.

Limits

What we are not.

Not a hardware company

No beacons, kiosks or sensors are required. If a venue wants them, they are scoped separately.

Not a security system

We do not connect cameras, dispatch guards or manage incidents. That is a different product.

Not a portfolio rollout yet

One venue is live. We do not claim multi-site deployments or results we have not measured.

Not accessibility-certified

Accessible routes are designed and tested in-house, with visually impaired input. No third-party certification is claimed.

Work with us.

We are looking for venue operators who want to test this properly, and for partners working on accessibility in African cities.
