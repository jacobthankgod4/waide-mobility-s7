About the company

About Waide Mobility

We source medical, mobility and orthopaedic supplies to order, and make maps and digital tools that help people find their way through buildings and help teams manage building information. Across seven business areas, Waide is based in Lagos and works with clients elsewhere.

Purpose

The physical world is under-described.

A building may be used for years without one up-to-date map of its rooms, entrances, services and equipment. That information is often spread across drawings, spreadsheets and staff knowledge.

That gap has practical consequences. Visitors cannot find a department. A facilities team cannot locate an asset. A planner cannot compare sites. An accessibility audit cannot see the routes people actually take.

Waide's purpose is to close that gap with connected building information that is easy to search and maintain.

Focus

Two divisions, seven business areas.

01

Supplies

Medical consumables and equipment, mobility equipment and aids, and orthopaedic supports, sourced to a written specification for hospitals, NGOs, schools and public buyers.

02

Indoor mapping

Building and site maps, searchable building records, web maps and tools, and step-free routes that work with screen readers.

03

Sector work

Tourism, hospitality, and construction and infrastructure: venue and site mapping, wayfinding, and the information that keeps both useful.

Software engineering, automation and computer vision are enabling capabilities in this work. They are not the company's primary identity, and we do not present them as the service itself.

On the supplies side there is no held stock or standing price list; quotations follow a written specification. See all seven business areas, the supplies division or the mapping services.

Approach

Small, senior and accountable.

Waide is deliberately small. The person who scopes a project is involved in delivering it, and the technical record is written by the people who did the work.

That model has limits: we take on a narrow set of assignments and we say no when the scope does not fit. It also has advantages. There is no handover between a sales team and a delivery team, no junior analyst learning the venue at the client's expense, and no distance between the data and the decision.

Inclusion

Accessibility is a design constraint, not a feature.

Maps and directions should work for people with different access needs. We plan step-free routes and screen-reader support as part of the project, not as add-ons.

We model step-free routes as a separate network, attach access conditions to facilities, and build text-first interfaces alongside map views. Where a project allows, we test with disabled users and record what changed as a result.

Technology and research interests

Where we are investing time.

  • Indoor data models that survive tenant churn and refits.
  • Accessible routing that treats level changes as first-class data.
  • Map checks that someone can run without specialist software training.
  • Low-bandwidth, browser-first interfaces for public venues.
  • BIM and GIS integration where the source models support it.
  • Open formats and exit routes that avoid platform lock-in.

Operating identity

What we are and are not.

  • A multi-sector company based in Lagos, Nigeria, working across seven business areas.
  • A specialist in medical and mobility supplies sourcing, and in geospatial and digital systems for the built environment.
  • Not a general-purpose software agency or a SaaS company.
  • Not a recruitment or personal CV site.
  • Not an approved supplier for any specific institution, and not a certification body. Waide Mobility Ltd is a registered company, RC 8283881; registration is legal identity, not an endorsement.
  • Not a company that publishes client work without permission.

Vision

Useful building maps as standard information.

Our aim is for building owners to keep usable maps and access information as standard, alongside plans for utilities and fire safety.

That means data models that can be handed over, methods that can be taught, and systems that do not depend on one supplier to stay correct. It also means accessibility information being part of the base dataset rather than a separate project.

Waide is early in that work. The completed Novare Lekki Mall project is our reference, not a finished portfolio. We would rather state that plainly than imply a scale we have not reached.
