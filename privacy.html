Privacy notice

How we handle personal data sent through this website, by email, or while using the third-party accessibility widget.

Last updated: 23 November 2026

At a glance

Short version.

The contact form sends your details to our own server endpoint, which emails the enquiry to our sales inbox and keeps it with the enquiry record. You get a reference number when it is received. We use the details to answer your enquiry. We do not sell personal data, run advertising, or use analytics cookies on this site.

This notice explains the detail. It is written for the way this site actually works, not as a generic template.

Who is responsible for your data

Waide Mobility, Lagos, Nigeria, is responsible for personal data collected through this website and through enquiry email. You can contact us at sales@waidemobility.com.

We have not appointed a statutory data protection officer. Privacy questions go to the same address and are handled by the founder.

What we collect and why

Contact and quotation enquiries

If you use the contact form, it asks for your name, organisation, email address, enquiry type, subject and message, plus a consent checkbox. On submission the fields are posted to our enquiry endpoint, which stores a record (with a reference number, the date, the page the enquiry came from and the browser user agent) and sends a notification email to the procurement inbox. The record exists so that an enquiry is not lost when the visitor's computer has no mail application. If the endpoint is unavailable, the page asks you to email us instead and does not store anything.

The website also records a short-lived technical counter against the connection that submits the form, so repeated automated submissions can be refused. It is not used to identify you or to build a profile.

When you email us, we receive your email address, name, organisation (if you give it), message and any attachments. We use this to answer the enquiry, prepare a quotation or tender response, and keep a record of the commercial relationship.

Hosting and security logs

Our hosting provider records standard technical information when a page is requested, such as IP address, user agent, requested URL and time. This is used to deliver the site, prevent abuse and diagnose faults.

Accessibility preferences

The accessibility widget is a third-party script loaded from jsDelivr. It may store your display preferences in your browser’s local storage so they persist between visits. Those preferences stay on your device. We do not read them and do not use them to identify you.

Third-party requests

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts and the accessibility widget from jsDelivr. Those providers receive the technical request data needed to serve the files, including your IP address and user agent, under their own privacy terms.

Legal bases

Where data protection law requires a legal basis, we rely on:

  • Legitimate interests in responding to business enquiries, preparing quotations, managing supplier and client relationships, and keeping the site secure.
  • Consent where you have given it, for example by ticking the consent box or asking to receive information. You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Contract where processing is needed to take steps you request before entering a contract, or to perform a contract with you.
  • Legal obligation where we must keep or disclose records under applicable law.

Cookies and local storage

Waide does not set advertising or analytics cookies on this site. The accessibility widget may use local storage for your chosen settings, as described above. You can clear that storage through your browser settings at any time; the widget will then return to its defaults.

How long we keep data

Enquiry emails and quotation records are kept for as long as needed to deal with the enquiry and maintain the business relationship. As a guide, we normally delete routine enquiry correspondence within 24 months of the last contact, unless a contract, dispute, legal obligation or procurement rule requires a longer period.

Enquiry records held in our database are kept on the same basis as enquiry email and are deleted or anonymised when no longer needed. Hosting logs are kept for the short period set by the hosting provider for security and troubleshooting.

Who we share data with

We do not sell personal data. We share it only where necessary with:

  • Email, hosting, database and IT service providers that operate the site, store enquiry records and deliver our correspondence. These currently include our hosting provider, our database provider and our transactional email provider.
  • Suppliers or logistics partners, where needed to prepare or deliver a quotation you have asked for.
  • Professional advisers, auditors or insurers, under confidentiality obligations.
  • Public authorities, courts or regulators where the law requires it.

Some providers may process data outside Nigeria. Where that happens, we rely on the provider’s contractual safeguards and applicable transfer rules.

Your rights

Depending on the law that applies to you, you may ask to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct data that is inaccurate or incomplete.
  • Delete data we no longer need, subject to legal retention duties.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Receive data you gave us in a portable format.
  • Withdraw consent where processing relies on it.

Email sales@waidemobility.com. We aim to respond within 30 days. You may also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) or to your local data protection authority.

Security

We use encrypted connections for this site, access controls on the accounts that hold correspondence, and routine software updates. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

This site is intended for organisations and professional buyers. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice when the site or our practices change. The date at the top shows the latest version. Material changes will be reflected here before they take effect.

Contact

Questions about this notice or a data request: sales@waidemobility.com.
