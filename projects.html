How we mapped Novare Lekki Mall

We mapped three levels of the mall in Lekki, from the car park to the shops. The map includes walking and vehicle routes, store locations, 900 Shoprite shelf locations and step-free paths.

The project

Novare Lekki Mall, mapped in detail.

The project follows a visitor from the car park into the mall and on to a store or supermarket shelf.

Building map

Stores and corridors

More than 150 shops, corridors, entrances, lifts and service areas mapped from plans and checked on site.

Routes

Walking and parking

Separate routes for people on foot and vehicles using more than 1,000 parking bays.

Store shelves

900 Shoprite shelves

We recorded 900 shelf locations, down to the aisle and shelf.

Step-free access

Routes without stairs

Routes using lifts and level entrances, checked on site.
