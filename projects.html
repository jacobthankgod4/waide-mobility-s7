Pilot in progress · flagship

Indoor spatial mapping and digital twin development, Novare Lekki Mall.

A three-level retail environment in Lekki, Lagos, digitised into a routable indoor spatial model with separate pedestrian and vehicle networks, shelf-level indexing and step-free routes.

What the Novare work contains

A single venue, mapped in depth.

The pilot covers the full route a visitor takes: from a parking bay, through a level change, into the retail floor, to a named store or a shelf inside the anchor supermarket.

Geometry

Units and circulation

More than 150 store footprints, corridors, entrances, lifts and service areas digitised from plans and checked against the building.

Networks

Pedestrian and vehicle

Two separate graphs: one for people on foot, one for vehicles using more than 1,000 parking bays.

Retail depth

900 Shoprite shelves

Shelf positions indexed so a search can resolve to an aisle and a shelf rather than a store entrance.

Access

Step-free routes

Accessible alternatives built from lifts and level access, validated on site. Not a third-party audit.

Completed work

What exists today

  • Digitised floor geometry for the three mapped levels.
  • Store footprints and anchor-supermarket shelf records.
  • Separate pedestrian and vehicle routing networks.
  • Step-free route layer with on-site validation notes.
  • A structured spatial dataset suitable for a routing interface.

Under development

What is not claimed as complete

  • Published pilot outcome metrics such as route completion rates.
  • Sensor or building-system integration.
  • Full production platform beyond the pilot scope.
  • Third-party accessibility certification.
  • Replication at other venues.

These are stated as development areas so that no reader mistakes a plan for a delivered result.
