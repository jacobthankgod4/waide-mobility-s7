Service divisions

01

Geospatial engineering & mapping

GIS mapping, indoor and outdoor spatial data collection, CAD and DWG to GIS conversion, georeferencing, feature extraction, spatial database development, point-of-interest mapping and network modelling.

For: estate owners, facilities teams, planning units and engineering firms that need a reliable spatial dataset.

→ 02

Digital twins & built-asset information

Multi-floor digital twins, layout digitisation, space and asset inventories, indoor spatial models, facility information systems and BIM/GIS integration where the source data supports it.

For: building owners and operators who need one structured description of what exists, where it is and how it connects.

→ 03

GIS platforms & geospatial software

Custom web GIS applications, mobile mapping interfaces, spatial APIs, PostGIS database architecture, dashboards, data integration and role-based access.

For: organisations that have the data and need a working interface, not another spreadsheet.

→ 04

Accessible mobility & wayfinding

Indoor navigation, accessible route mapping, step-free network information, accessible point-of-interest data, audio guidance and screen-reader-compatible interfaces.

For: transport, healthcare, education, retail and public facilities that need to guide people through complex space.

→ 05

Technical advisory & capacity building

Geospatial requirements analysis, GIS implementation advisory, spatial data-management frameworks, quality assurance, technical training and documentation.

For: institutions and consultancies that need an independent technical position before they commit budget.

How work is commissioned

Four engagement shapes.

The scope note for each engagement states the deliverables, the acceptance criteria and what is explicitly excluded.

Assessment

Data and feasibility review

A short study of existing plans, spatial records and systems, with a gap register and a recommended route to delivery.

Mapping

Spatial dataset build

Digitisation, georeferencing, attribute structuring, route networks and validation, delivered as a documented dataset.

Platform

Geospatial application

Database, API and interface built around one operational decision, deployed and handed over with documentation.

Advisory

Technical oversight

Requirements, specifications, procurement support, QA and capacity building alongside your own team or supplier.

Waide is based in Lagos, Nigeria, and works remotely with clients elsewhere. Travel and site work are scoped explicitly rather than assumed.

Where it applies

Buildings and infrastructure where spatial information has to work.

Malls, markets and large stores: tenant mapping, shelf-level search, parking networks and visitor routing.

Hospitals and clinics: department wayfinding, step-free routes, accessible facilities and front-desk load.

Transport

Airports, stations and interchanges: multi-level routing, gates, platforms, facilities and accessible connections.

Infrastructure programmes

GIS data structures, QA frameworks and advisory support for programmes that need a spatial evidence base.

Questions

Before you commission a service.

Do you work outside Nigeria?

Yes, remotely, and on site where travel is agreed in the scope. We do not claim offices or operations in countries where we have none.

Can you work from drawings only?

Often, yes. If the drawings are out of date or incomplete, a measured survey or field check is added as a separate, quoted task.

Do you provide the software as well as the data?

Where the scope includes a platform, yes: database, interface and documentation. Where it does not, we deliver the dataset and specification so another team can build on it.

Is accessibility certification included?

No. We implement and test accessible routes and interfaces, and document what was tested. Formal certification requires an accredited third party and is outside our scope.

How do projects start?

With a short scoping conversation, then a written scope note covering deliverables, exclusions, assumptions, acceptance criteria and cost.
