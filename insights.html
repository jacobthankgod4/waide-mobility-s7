Editorial policy

Why there are only three notes.

We publish when there is something specific to say. A technical note is written by the person who did the work, reviewed against the project record, and left unpublished if the evidence is not there.

  • No generated volume to fill a blog index.
  • No invented authors, dates or citations.
  • No client material published without permission.
  • No claims that a pilot result has been measured when it has not.

Where a note refers to the Novare Lekki pilot, it describes method and scope. Operational outcomes from that pilot have not been published.
