How data comes in

Three inputs, checked by a person.

  1. Plans

    The building files you already have. We check them against a walkthrough before drawing anything.

  2. Directory

    Unit names, tenants, departments and hours. One list, owned by the venue, with a named person who can approve changes.

  3. Product data

    Only if product-level search is in scope. We need names, categories and shelf locations in a format we can index.

Adding one

What an integration would take.

01

A real use case

Not "connect everything". One decision the integration would change, such as reconciling footfall with sales.

02

An owner on your side

Someone who can grant access, answer questions about the system and test the result.

03

A test window

A pilot period with a defined output. If it cannot be tested in a pilot, we will not promise it in a contract.

Questions

Common questions

Can you connect to our POS?

Not today. The reporting is delivered from Waide and can be exported as CSV. A POS integration would be a separate project, and we would tell you if we thought it was worth doing.

Do we need to open a firewall port?

No. Nothing connects to your network. Plans and lists are sent as files, and reports are delivered from Waide.

Can we get the raw data out?

Yes, as a CSV export of the weekly report. The venue owns the data about its building.

Is there an API for our developers?

No public API or SDK today. If your team needs one, raise it during scoping and we will say whether it exists or not.

Will you sign an integration roadmap?

No. We will describe what is running now and quote a specific piece of work if you need something else. We do not sign dates for features that are not built.

Name the system.

Tell us what you use and what decision the connection would change. We will answer with a yes, a no, or a scoped piece of work.
