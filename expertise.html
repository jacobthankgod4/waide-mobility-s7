People and experience

Meet the people behind Waide

Waide is led by its founder and brings in specialists when a project needs them. We list people and experience only with permission and describe each person's role accurately.

Founder

ThankGod Jacob Chibueze

Founder & Lead Geospatial and Digital Systems Consultant

ThankGod leads Waide's mapping and digital-tool work. His experience includes GIS, indoor maps, software and accessibility, with a focus on tools client teams can maintain after handover.

He built and led the Novare Lekki Mall indoor mapping pilot, aligning the mall plans with the map, adding walking and vehicle routes, mapping supermarket shelves and checking step-free paths. He also works on browser maps and screen-reader-friendly controls.

His background spans full-stack engineering, technical programme leadership and assistive technology. That combination is deliberate: spatial systems fail as often at the interface and the handover as they do at the data layer.

Working model

Senior involvement, not delegation to a junior team.

The founder takes part in both planning and delivering each project. This keeps decisions close to the work while the team is small.

Where a project needs additional capacity, we name the collaborator, define their contribution and keep technical accountability with Waide. We do not present subcontracted work as in-house capability.

What we will not claim

  • Team members we cannot name with consent.
  • Qualifications or certifications not held.
  • Client references not given for publication.
  • Project outcomes not measured.
  • Offices or operations in countries where we have none.
