Transparency

Evidence and claims register

We publish the basis for the numbers on this site. Where a figure is measured, we say so. Where it is a model or an industry estimate, we say that too — and explain what it would take to prove it.

Last reviewed: 14 November 2026

How to read the labels

Every claim on the site should carry one of these four labels. If a figure appears without one, treat it as an estimate until we publish the basis.

Measured Observed Model Industry estimate
  • Measured — counted or surveyed by Waide during a deployment. Scope and date are stated.
  • Observed — a qualitative pattern seen in the field, not a statistically controlled result.
  • Model — a calculation from stated assumptions. Changing the assumptions changes the number.
  • Industry estimate — a range used across retail wayfinding, not independently verified for this market by Waide.

How the US$5–7M figure is calculated

The figure is an illustrative model for a large venue. It is not a claim about any specific property, including Novare Lekki.

lost sales ≈ annual venue sales × share of shoppers lost to navigation × average basket value recovered

example: US$40M × 15% = US$6.0M
example: US$45M × 15% = US$6.75M

Assumptions: the venue turns over roughly US$35–45M a year; 15–20% of shoppers leave because they cannot find an item; those shoppers would otherwise complete a purchase at the venue’s average basket value. The model does not account for shoppers who substitute another product, so it may overstate the recoverable amount. We treat it as a ceiling for discussion, not a forecast.

Do not quote the US$5–7M figure as a measured result. It is a model built on an unverified estimate. The pilot is designed to replace it with venue-specific evidence.

What we do not claim

  • We do not claim that the Novare Lekki pilot has already produced a measured increase in sales or conversion.
  • We do not claim commercial rollout beyond the pilot, or deployments at venues that are not listed on this site.
  • We do not claim third-party certification of our accessible routing. It is designed and tested in-house, with input from visually impaired co-designers.
  • We do not claim independent audit of zone performance or anchor spillover data. Those reports are generated from Waide’s own platform data.
  • We do not claim that any single percentage applies uniformly to every venue, market or format.

What the pilot will measure

The six-month evaluation is structured to produce evidence that can survive scrutiny from an asset manager, a lender or a fund board.

  • Search-to-destination completion rate, by venue zone and product category.
  • Navigation demand versus point-of-sale conversion at shelf and category level.
  • Zone traffic rankings and corridor flow, reported weekly.
  • Anchor spillover: measured movement from anchor zones to adjacent units.
  • Repeat use and pre-visit inventory checks, where inventory integration is enabled.
  • Accessibility route usage and completion.

Results will be published in summary form when the evaluation closes, with venue-sensitive commercial detail removed. Until then, any forward-looking figure on this site is a model.
